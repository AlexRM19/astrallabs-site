Lexio

Learn English from the content you actually read — your articles, your books, subtitles from videos you watch — instead of someone else's word lists.

How it works

You read a text inside the app and tap any word you don't know. It goes into your deck with the sentence it came from, because context is most of what makes a word stick. From there the word comes back on a spaced-repetition schedule (FSRS): first just to recognise, then as a gap in a sentence, then typed from the translation, and finally by ear. A word you can read but can't hear or produce isn't learned yet, and the app treats it that way.

What's in it

  • 01A placement test that measures grammar and vocabulary separately, so you get a profile rather than one letter.
  • 02A built-in shelf of public-domain books, labelled A1 to C1, so there is something to read from the first minute.
  • 03Your own files: EPUB, FB2, TXT and PDF, with chapters and images preserved.
  • 04YouTube subtitles and learner podcasts, with the text synced to the audio.
  • 05A dictionary of over 20,000 entries with IPA transcription, bundled in the app.
  • 06Grammar lessons that follow from the mistakes you actually make.
  • 07Backup and restore of everything you've learned, as a single file.

Works offline

The dictionary, the book shelf and your whole deck live on the device. On a plane or in a metro tunnel the app behaves exactly as it does at home. It needs the network only to fetch something new: subtitles, a podcast episode or a book you asked for.

No account, no tracking, no ads. Lexio has no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising code of any kind. Everything you do stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Availability

Lexio is on Android, currently in testing on Google Play. The interface is in Russian. Write to contact@astrallabs.eu if you would like to join the test.
