Software that ships and keeps working

Astral Labs is a software studio in Wrocław, Poland. We design and build mobile apps, web services and embedded software — for clients who need a product delivered, and as products of our own.

Start a project See our apps

What we do

01

Mobile apps

Android and iOS apps, in Flutter or native code, taken from an idea to a published release. We handle the parts that usually slow a launch down: offline-first data, store compliance, privacy policies and Data Safety forms, staged rollouts and updates after release.

02

Web

Landing pages, web applications, admin dashboards and the APIs behind them. From a fast static site with a custom domain and HTTPS to a full-stack product with authentication, payments and a database — sized to what the project actually needs.

03

Embedded & IoT

Firmware for microcontrollers and Linux-based devices, connectivity over BLE, Wi-Fi and MQTT, companion mobile apps and cloud integration. We are comfortable across the whole chain, from a sensor reading on the board to a chart on a phone screen.

How we work

  • 01You talk directly to the engineers doing the work, not to an account manager.
  • 02A short discovery first: we agree on scope, milestones and what “done” means before anyone writes code.
  • 03Fixed-price for well-defined scope, time and materials for evolving products; either way, weekly progress you can see and run.
  • 04Code, documentation and infrastructure access are yours from day one. No lock-in.
  • 05Privacy by default: we collect only the data a product needs and say so plainly in its policy.
